# Rolling-Memory Brand Assets

**[English](README.md) · [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)**

The wordmark stacks three capital **R** letters left-to-right — **Remember · Rolling · Recall** — with
left-heavy weight and right-light weight.

Geometry: a fully open counter and leg on the first letter (L0), a `4.5u` optical cutout, a `5.2u`
anchored stencil notch on the left column, and louvered rasters on the two rear layers.

Four standard editions: **① Positive** (dark ink on paper white) · **② Inverted** (pure white on
obsidian) · **③ Rainbow positive** (high-density R7 aurora on paper white) · **④ Rainbow inverted**
(high-key R7 aurora on obsidian).

## 1. Three Wordmark Families

| Family | Wordmark | Core geometry | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **`F03`** | **Open WebUI · Archivo Black** (curved-knee stencil louver) | `23u` left column + `5.2u` vertical notch + curved right leg, three stacked layers forming three parallel S-curves | Modern AI platforms, chat and plugin surfaces |
| **`P03`** | **Prism 45° · faceted prism** (octagonal notch louver) | `24u` left column + `5.2u` vertical notch + `45°` optical chamfer octagonal bowl + `28°` straight-cut leg | Memory engine infrastructure and technical material |
| **`R07`** | **Grotesk Stencil · 01 industrial geometry** (round-bowl notch louver) | `24u` left column + `5.2u` vertical notch + `R30/R13` optically offset inner ring + `28°` straight-cut leg | General engineering marks, full-size reference |
